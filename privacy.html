Privacy policy · English

Emmanuel is the app of Emmanuel Chinese Christian Church, 108 Hillcrest Road, Warren, NJ 07059. Translation Services USA LLC publishes it on the App Store for the church.

The short version

Version 1.0 has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your phone is what you type into the Bible assistant (Ask): it goes to OpenAI to write the answer, together with the first name and “man” or “woman” if you chose to give them, but never an account or a device identifier.

The Bible assistant

When you ask a question, the app sends OpenAI your question, the recent conversation on screen, the app's language and, if you gave them, your first name and whether you are a man or a woman (so the answer can address you, for example as Grace or 美玲姊妹), and receives the answer. Both are optional; you can change or remove them in Settings. OpenAI handles them under its API terms and privacy policy (openai.com/policies). The conversation is kept only on your phone, and “Clear conversation” deletes it. Please do not type other people's names, health details or anything private. The assistant asks for your birth year once and is for ages 13 and up; the year stays on your phone.

What the app contains

The Bible in three editions, the church's reading plan, service times, the church's address and how to give through Zelle are all built into the app. They work without an internet connection.

What stays on your phone

Your choice of language, your conversation with the assistant, your birth year, the first name and “man” or “woman” you gave the assistant, whether the startup sound is on, and your agreement to use the assistant. Deleting the app removes all of it.

When you tap a link

Directions open Apple Maps, “Email the church” opens Mail, hotline numbers open Phone, “Add to calendar” opens Apple's own New Event screen, and “Invite a friend” opens the share sheet. Those apps follow their own privacy policies. The app does not read your calendar, photos, contacts or location.

Giving

The app only explains how to give with Zelle in your own bank's app. It never processes, sees or records a gift.

Children

The Bible assistant is not available to children under 13, and the rest of the app collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a later version adds features that need information, such as sign-in, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's privacy details on the App Store will change too.

Questions

Write to ecccnj@gmail.com.
