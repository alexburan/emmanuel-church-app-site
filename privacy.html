Privacy policy · English

Emmanuel is the app of Emmanuel Chinese Christian Church, 108 Hillcrest Road, Warren, NJ 07059. Translation Services USA LLC publishes it on the App Store for the church.

The short version

Version 1.0 has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Only two things ever leave your phone: what you type into the Bible assistant (Ask), and, if you turn on translation in Live captions, the written sentences to be translated. Both go to the app's own server, run by Translation Services USA LLC, the app's publisher (or, only when that server is unavailable, to OpenAI), together with the first name and “man” or “woman” if you chose to give them to the assistant, but never an account or a device identifier. The app's server keeps nothing once the answer is written; OpenAI handles text it receives under its API terms and privacy policy (openai.com/policies). Speech never leaves your phone.

The Bible assistant

When you ask a question, the app sends the app's own server your question, the recent conversation on screen, the app's language and, if you gave them, your first name and whether you are a man or a woman (so the answer can address you, for example as Grace or 美玲姊妹), and receives the answer. Both are optional; you can change or remove them in Settings. The server, run by Translation Services USA LLC, the app's publisher, writes the answer, keeps nothing afterwards and shares nothing with anyone else. If that server is unavailable, the same text goes to OpenAI instead, which handles it under its API terms and privacy policy (openai.com/policies). The conversation is kept only on your phone, and “New chat” on the Ask screen deletes it. Please do not type other people's names, health details or anything private. The assistant asks for your birth year once and is for ages 13 and up; the year stays on your phone.

Live captions and translation

Live captions use your iPhone's microphone only while the captions screen is open and listening. The speech recognition built into your iPhone turns the speech into text on the phone itself; the audio is not sent to Apple or anyone else. When Save recording is on, the audio and the text are kept only on this phone until you delete them. Nothing leaves the phone unless you export it. If you turn on translation, the written sentences (not the audio) are sent to the app's own server to be translated (or to OpenAI when that server is unavailable), without any name, account or device identifier, and the app's server does not keep them. Saved recordings and transcripts stay on your phone unless you export or share them. Please ask before you caption a private conversation.

What the app contains

The Bible in three editions, the church's reading plan, service times, the church's address and how to give through Zelle are all built into the app. They work without an internet connection.

What stays on your phone

Your choice of language, your conversation with the assistant, your birth year, the first name and “man” or “woman” you gave the assistant, whether the startup sound is on, your agreement to use the assistant, your saved sermon recordings and caption transcripts, the minutes translated today, and how many days you have used the app (only so Apple's rating request appears at a good moment). Deleting the app removes all of it.

When you tap a link

Directions open Apple Maps, “Email the church” opens Mail, hotline numbers open Phone, “Add to calendar” opens Apple's own New Event screen, “Invite a friend” opens the share sheet, “Rate the app” opens the App Store and “Send feedback” opens Mail. Those apps follow their own privacy policies. The app does not read your calendar, photos, contacts or location, and uses the microphone only for Live captions.

Giving

The app only explains how to give with Zelle in your own bank's app. It never processes, sees or records a gift.

Children

The Bible assistant and Live captions are for ages 13 and up, and the rest of the app collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a later version adds features that need information, such as sign-in, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's privacy details on the App Store will change too.

Questions

Write to ecccnj@gmail.com.
